What Is the Average Cost of a Wedding in Tuscany?

A realistic guide to Tuscany wedding budgets, from local celebrations to international destination weddings, with the cost drivers couples should compare.

9 min read  ·  WeddingCoding Editorial

Tuscany is one of Italy’s best-known destination wedding regions, but there is no single average that applies to every event. A countryside villa for a multi-day celebration, a city ceremony in Florence and an intimate dinner at a restaurant are different products with different costs.

What do the available figures tell us?

Published estimates vary because they may cover different types of weddings. Destination Florence, citing wedding-sector research, has reported average spending of around €68,600 per event in a destination-wedding context. A separate Tuscany wedding-sector report for 2025 described an average event budget around €42,900 for a broader event sample. These figures are not directly comparable: scope, guest profile and event definition matter. Treat them as indicators of market variation, not a guaranteed quote for your wedding.

Why Tuscany can cost more or less than expected

The region includes Florence and other cities, hilltop villages, vineyards, agriturismi, historic estates, luxury villas and coastal areas. Costs vary with exclusivity, overnight accommodation, guest capacity, access, catering arrangements, required rentals and whether suppliers must travel. A venue that appears less expensive may require significant external rentals or transport.

The main budget categories

Venue hire and accommodation; food and drinks; planning and coordination; photography and video; floral design and styling; music and entertainment; ceremony and celebrant; guest transport; stationery and digital communication; hair and make-up; attire and rings; and a contingency fund. Ask whether venue fees include furniture, staffing, cleaning, power, sound limits, setup and next-day breakdown.

Guest count and number of days

A 30-person wedding may feel very different from a 120-person event. Per-person catering costs rise with the guest count, while many supplier fees are fixed. A welcome dinner, pool party or farewell brunch adds another layer of food, staff, transport and rentals. When comparing budgets, specify whether the figure covers one day or the entire wedding weekend.

Season and location

Late spring and early autumn are popular, so attractive dates may book early and command higher prices. July and August can involve heat and high tourism demand. Off-season dates can offer a different atmosphere and sometimes greater availability, but confirm venue opening schedules, heating, rain plans and local services.

Questions to ask a Tuscan venue

Is exclusive use included? Are accommodation and breakfast included? What are the minimum spend and event end time? Are outside caterers permitted? Is there a plan for rain or extreme heat? What are the noise rules? Are there vendor restrictions, cleaning fees, taxes, deposits or damage deposits? Is transport needed for guests staying elsewhere?

Build a useful comparison

Compare three venues using the same guest count, event schedule, catering assumptions, accommodation needs and supplier scope. Separate venue price from total event cost. Keep estimates and confirmed commitments distinct, then review the forecast whenever a major choice changes.

Make the guest journey part of the plan

International guests benefit from clear travel guidance, a schedule, accommodation suggestions and updates in one place. A wedding website, digital invitations, guest chatbot or digital seating plan can make information easier to manage without replacing the human care that makes a celebration memorable.

A realistic conclusion

For Tuscany, use market averages only to frame early conversations. Your true budget should be based on the actual venue, guest count, date, service level and number of events. Request itemised quotes before committing, and retain a contingency for changes.
