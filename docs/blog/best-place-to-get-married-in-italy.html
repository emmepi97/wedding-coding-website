Where Is the Best Place to Get Married in Italy?

Compare Tuscany, Lake Como, the Amalfi Coast, Puglia, Venice and Italian cities to find a wedding destination that fits your style and guests.

10 min read  ·  WeddingCoding Editorial

The best place to get married in Italy is the place that matches your priorities: landscape, travel access, guest count, budget, ceremony style and the experience you want to share. Italy offers very different settings, so start with the feeling you want rather than choosing a famous location first.

Tuscany: countryside, villas and vineyards

Tuscany suits couples imagining rolling hills, historic estates, vineyards, olive groves and multi-day celebrations. It can work well for a wedding weekend where guests stay nearby and share several events. Check travel times between airports, hotels and the venue, and remember that rural locations may need shuttle transport.

Lake Como: dramatic scenery and elegant villas

Lake Como is known for lakeside views, historic villas and a refined atmosphere. It can be ideal for smaller or premium celebrations, but boat transfers, access, guest accommodation and venue restrictions may have a significant effect on the budget. Ask about weather contingencies and the practicalities of moving guests between locations.

Amalfi Coast: sea views and Mediterranean character

The Amalfi Coast offers dramatic coastal scenery, colourful towns and a strong holiday feel. Narrow roads, seasonal traffic, stairs and limited parking can complicate logistics. Consider how guests will travel, whether transfers are needed and whether the venue is accessible for everyone invited.

Puglia: masserie, olive groves and warm evenings

Puglia is associated with traditional masserie, stone architecture, olive groves and relaxed outdoor dining. It can suit couples who want a distinctive southern Italian atmosphere. Review airport connections, accommodation capacity, heat management and transport if the venue is outside a town.

Venice: history and unmistakable atmosphere

Venice offers canals, palaces, historic architecture and a distinctive city experience. Water transport, luggage, accessibility and movement through busy public areas need careful coordination. It can be a strong option for couples who prioritise atmosphere and a city celebration over a countryside retreat.

Florence and other Italian cities

Florence offers art, architecture and urban convenience, while Rome, Verona, Siena and other cities bring their own history and character. City weddings may simplify some guest logistics but can require careful planning around traffic, public events, venue access and accommodation.

How to choose between destinations

Rank your priorities: scenery, legal ceremony options, budget, guest travel, venue exclusivity, accommodation, accessibility, weather plan and activities for a wedding weekend. Ask guests to travel to one central area where possible. Compare the full experience, not only the ceremony photograph.

Think beyond the ceremony location

The guest journey includes airport arrival, transfers, hotel check-in, welcome events, ceremony, dinner and departure. A wedding website can make the itinerary and travel advice easier to understand; digital invitations can collect responses; a chatbot can answer approved FAQs; and a digital seating chart can help guests find their table. These details make a beautiful destination easier to experience.

A simple shortlist method

Choose three regions, identify two or three venues in each, and request comparable information for the same guest count and event schedule. Visit if possible or arrange a detailed virtual tour. Confirm all important terms in writing before paying deposits.
