A few useful answers

Frequently asked
questions.

Who are WeddingCoding services for?

Our digital services are designed for couples planning a destination wedding in Italy, including international couples, as well as wedding planners and teams who want to offer a more organised digital experience.

Can we use only one service?

Yes. You can start with a single service, such as a wedding website or digital invitation, and explore additional tools only if they are useful for your celebration.

Can the website include travel information for international guests?

Yes. A wedding website can bring together event details, maps, travel guidance, accommodation suggestions, dress code, schedules and RSVP information in one accessible place.

Can services be personalised to our wedding style?

Our aim is to tailor the content and experience to your story, destination and visual preferences. The available scope and timing can be discussed based on the service you choose.

Do you work with wedding planners?

Yes. We can discuss how digital services may support a planner's workflow or become part of the experience they offer to couples.

How do we get started?

Explore the demos, then contact us with your wedding date, destination and the services you are considering. We will use that information to discuss the most suitable next step.
