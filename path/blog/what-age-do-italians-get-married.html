At What Age Do Italians Get Married?

Explore the latest official figures on the average age at first marriage in Italy and the social trends behind couples marrying later.

6 min read  ·  WeddingCoding Editorial

Italians tend to marry later than previous generations. According to Italy’s national statistics institute, ISTAT, the average age at first marriage in 2024 was 34.8 for men and 32.8 for women. These are national averages, not a rule for individual couples.

What do the latest official figures say?

ISTAT’s report on marriages in 2024, published in January 2026, records an average age at first marriage of 34.8 years for men and 32.8 years for women. In 2011, the corresponding averages were 32.6 and 30.1, showing a longer-term shift toward later first marriages.

Why are Italians marrying later?

There is no single explanation. Longer education, difficulty entering or establishing oneself in the labour market, housing costs, economic uncertainty and the growth of cohabitation before marriage can all influence timing. Demographic change also affects the number of people in younger age groups. These factors vary by region and personal circumstances.

Does the average mean most couples marry at that age?

No. An average compresses many different life paths into one number. Some people marry in their twenties; others marry in their forties or later, remarry, or choose not to marry. Age at first marriage is also different from the age at any subsequent marriage.

What does this mean for destination weddings?

Many couples planning a destination wedding are coordinating careers, finances, family travel and multiple households. A clear timeline and shared decision-making process can be as valuable as the venue itself. A wedding website, digital invitations and a central planning dashboard help keep the details visible to the couple and their guests.

Planning around your own timeline

Start with the date that makes sense for you, then work backwards from venue availability, travel needs and supplier lead times. Avoid comparing your choices with a national average: your priorities, relationships and circumstances are personal.

A note on the data

The figures above refer to average age at first marriage in Italy in 2024 as reported by ISTAT. They should not be confused with the average age of everyone who married that year, which can include remarriages and different demographic groups.
