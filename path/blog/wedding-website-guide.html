What Should a Destination Wedding Website Include?

The essential pages and details for a useful destination wedding website, especially when guests are travelling internationally.

WeddingCoding Editorial · Practical guide

For a destination wedding, a website is more than a digital invitation. It can become the reliable place guests return to for travel information, schedule updates and practical answers before they leave home.

The essentials guests look for

Make the date, location, ceremony and reception schedule, RSVP deadline and contact details easy to find. Avoid making guests search through long paragraphs for key information.

Travel and accommodation

Share the nearest airports or stations, suggested travel routes, accommodation areas and shuttle information when available. Clearly distinguish confirmed arrangements from recommendations.

A practical FAQ

Answer common questions about dress code, weather, children, dietary needs, gifts, plus-ones and local customs. Keep answers concise and update them when plans change.

Mobile-first and accessible design

Many guests will open the site from a phone. Use readable text, strong contrast, descriptive links and compressed images so the page loads reliably on mobile connections.

Privacy and updates

Only publish personal details that the couple is comfortable sharing. If information is sensitive, consider password protection or sharing details directly with invited guests.

Connect the website to the wider experience
